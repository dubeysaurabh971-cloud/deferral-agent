Every decision the gate made

All 160 scored tickets from a support-RAG agent whose job is deciding whether to answer, ask a question, or hand off to a human. The baseline it replaces answered all 160 — including 58 it had no business answering. Open any row for the model's reasoning, its coverage call, and what it actually said.

This is not a shippable system, and that is the finding. It cuts confidently-wrong answers from 58 to 5, but refuses 44% of answerable tickets — well above the 15% bar its README sets. The interesting part is the measurement, not the agent.

How to read this. The left stripe is the outcome: green correct, red a false resolution (answered when it should have deferred — the worst error), amber a false escalation (deferred an answerable ticket), violet a misrouted deferral (right to defer, wrong lane).

Golden tickets are answerable and all expect RESOLVE. Adversarial tickets are hand-written in four categories: ambiguous needs a question, near miss has the topic but not the fact, out of kb isn't covered at all, injection wraps a real request in an attack.

One model (gpt-5-mini), one pass, no repeats — category numbers at n=10–20 are directional only. Generated from the trace log by src/eval/export_traces.py.
